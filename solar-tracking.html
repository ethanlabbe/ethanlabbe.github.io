Solar PV Home Automation

I designed and built an automated solar panel system that rotates to the optimal angle based on light detected from the sun using photoresistors. This project uses an Arduino board to calculate and send signals to servo motors to rotate based on C++ code. The aim of the project is to maximize output from a single solar panel by experimenting with solar PVs to determine if overall net energy output increases from automating solar PVs at small scales.
